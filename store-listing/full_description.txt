FaithFlow is a local-first Android app for tracking your scripture life: verse themes, personal notes, and daily walk records, with cloud sync and offline support.

🚀 Features:
- Google Sign-In for secure cloud sync.
- Create and manage verse themes.
- Manual verse entry or import from the Android Share Sheet.
- Rich-text personal notes with Bible reference detection.
- Daily walk tracker to record reading, prayer, and insights.
- Local Bible database (bible.db) for quick verse lookup.
- In-app updates and tutorial overlay.

Developed as a Catholic-friendly tool for scripture study, prayer, and reflection.
